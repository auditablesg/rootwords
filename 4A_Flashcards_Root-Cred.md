# Lesson 4A: Root 'cred' (Trust/Believe) - Flashcards

## Card 1: CREDIT
**Front:** Credit (noun/verb)
**Back:** 
- **Meaning:** Recognition for achievement; a sum of money lent
- **Root:** cred (trust, believe)
- **Chinese:** 学分/信用 (xuéfen/xìnyòng)
- **Example:** The bank gave him a line of credit because they trusted his ability to repay the loan.

---

## Card 2: CREDIBLE
**Front:** Credible (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Worthy of trust; able to be believed
- **Root:** cred (trust) + -ible (able to)
- **Chinese:** 可信的 (kěxìn de)
- **Example:** A credible news source provides accurate, verified information from reliable journalists.

---

## Card 3: INCREDIBLE
**Front:** Incredible (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Amazing, extraordinary; almost unbelievable
- **Root:** in- (not) + cred (trust) + -ible (able)
- **Chinese:** 不可思议的 (búkě sīyì de)
- **Example:** The acrobat's incredible performance left the audience astounded and amazed.

---

## Card 4: CREDENTIAL
**Front:** Credential (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A document that proves qualifications or authority
- **Root:** cred (trust) + -ential
- **Chinese:** 证书/资格 (zhèngshū/zīgé)
- **Example:** The doctor presented her medical credential before beginning work at the hospital.

---

## Card 5: DISCREDIT
**Front:** Discredit (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To harm the reputation or credibility of someone
- **Root:** dis- (not/against) + credit (trust)
- **Chinese:** 损害信誉 (sǔnhài xìnyù)
- **Example:** False rumors can discredit an honest person's reputation in the community.

---

## Card 6: CREDITOR
**Front:** Creditor (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A person or organization that lends money
- **Root:** cred (trust) + -or (one who)
- **Chinese:** 债权人 (zhàiquán rén)
- **Example:** The creditor agreed to extend the payment deadline for three additional months.

---

## Card 7: INCREDULOUS
**Front:** Incredulous (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Unwilling to believe something; expressing disbelief
- **Root:** in- (not) + cred (trust) + -ulous (full of)
- **Chinese:** 怀疑的 (huáiyí de)
- **Example:** She gave him an incredulous look when he claimed to have finished all his homework in five minutes.

---

## Card 8: CREDULITY
**Front:** Credulity (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Willingness to believe things too easily without enough evidence
- **Root:** cred (trust) + -ity (quality)
- **Chinese:** 轻信 (qīngxìn)
- **Example:** His credulity made him an easy target for scams and false advertising schemes.

---

## Card 9: ACCREDIT
**Front:** Accredit (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To officially recognize as meeting standards or requirements
- **Root:** ac- (to) + credit (trust)
- **Chinese:** 认可/批准 (rènkě/pīzhǔn)
- **Example:** The university is accredit ed by the government to award degrees and certificates.

---

## Card 10: MISCREANT
**Front:** Miscreant (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A person who behaves badly; a villain or scoundrel
- **Root:** mis- (bad) + cre (believe/trust)
- **Chinese:** 恶棍 (èguǐn)
- **Example:** The miscreant was arrested for stealing from local shops.

---

## Card 11: CREDENCE
**Front:** Credence (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Belief in or acceptance of something as true
- **Root:** cred (trust/believe)
- **Chinese:** 相信/信任 (xiāngxìn/xìnrèn)
- **Example:** The old legend gained credence when archeologists found supporting evidence.

---

## Card 12: CREDO
**Front:** Credo (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A statement of the principles or beliefs of a person or group
- **Root:** credo = Latin for "I believe"
- **Chinese:** 信条 (xìntiáo)
- **Example:** The company's credo emphasizes integrity and customer satisfaction.

---

## Root Summary
- **Latin Root:** cred = trust, believe, faith
- **Key Prefixes:** in-, dis-, ac-, mis-
- **Key Suffixes:** -ible, -or, -ity, -ulous, -ence
- **Memory Tip:** Think of "credit" as trusting someone with money - all cred words relate to belief, trust, and credibility.
