GRAPH/GRAM Root Summary

GRAPH/GRAM (write, record, drawing) appears in words related to writing, visual representation, and documentation. A graph displays data visually. Geography literally means "writing about the earth." A biography is a written account of someone's life. Grammar is the rules for writing language. Autograph is your unique handwriting/signature. Understanding this root helps you recognize words about communication, writing, and visual documentation.
