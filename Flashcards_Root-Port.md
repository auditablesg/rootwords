# Lesson 3A: Root 'port' (Carry/Bear) - Flashcards

## Card 1: TRANSPORT
**Front:** Transport (noun/verb)
**Back:** 
- **Meaning:** To carry goods or people from one place to another
- **Root:** trans- (across) + port (carry)
- **Chinese:** 运输/运送 (yùnshù/yùnsòng)
- **Example:** Heavy cargo ships transport goods across the ocean between different countries.

---

## Card 2: EXPORT
**Front:** Export (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To send goods to another country for sale
- **Root:** ex- (out) + port (carry)
- **Chinese:** 出口 (chūkǒu)
- **Example:** Local farmers export fresh vegetables and fruits to overseas markets.

---

## Card 3: IMPORT
**Front:** Import (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To bring goods into a country from abroad
- **Root:** im- (in) + port (carry)
- **Chinese:** 进口 (jìnkǒu)
- **Example:** Singapore needs to import a large portion of its food and energy supplies.

---

## Card 4: SUPPORT
**Front:** Support (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To hold up or bear the weight of something; to help or back up
- **Root:** sup- (under) + port (carry)
- **Chinese:** 支持 (zhīchí)
- **Example:** Strong pillars are required to support the heavy weight of the roof structure.

---

## Card 5: REPORT
**Front:** Report (noun/verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** An account or statement of facts; to give an account of
- **Root:** re- (back) + port (carry)
- **Chinese:** 报告 (bàogào)
- **Example:** Journalists must verify their facts before publishing a news report.

---

## Card 6: PORTABLE
**Front:** Portable (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Able to be easily carried or moved
- **Root:** port (carry) + -able (able to)
- **Chinese:** 便携式/手提的 (biànxiéshì/shǒutí de)
- **Example:** Students find tablets useful because they are lightweight and portable.

---

## Card 7: AIRPORT
**Front:** Airport (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A facility where aircraft take off and land
- **Root:** air + port (carry/place)
- **Chinese:** 机场 (jīchǎng)
- **Example:** Travelers must arrive at the airport at least two hours before their flight departure.

---

## Card 8: PASSPORT
**Front:** Passport (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** An official document that allows a person to travel to foreign countries
- **Root:** pass (pass through) + port (carry/place)
- **Chinese:** 护照 (hùzhào)
- **Example:** Always keep your passport in a safe place while traveling abroad.

---

## Card 9: PORTER
**Front:** Porter (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A person whose job is to carry luggage at a hotel, station, or airport
- **Root:** port (carry) + -er (one who)
- **Chinese:** 搬运工 (bānyùngōng)
- **Example:** The porter helped us carry our heavy suitcases to our hotel room.

---

## Card 10: PORTFOLIO
**Front:** Portfolio (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A collection of work or investments; a case for carrying documents
- **Root:** port (carry) + folio (leaf/pages)
- **Chinese:** 作品集/投资组合 (zuòpǐnjí/tóuzī zǔhé)
- **Example:** Artists often display their portfolio to potential clients.

---

## Card 11: IMPORTANT
**Front:** Important (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Of great significance, value, or consequence; mattering greatly
- **Root:** im- (in) + port (carry) + -ant (full of)
- **Chinese:** 重要的 (zhòngyào de)
- **Example:** Time management is an important skill for students preparing for examinations.

---

## Card 12: DEPORT
**Front:** Deport (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To expel a person from a country, usually by official order
- **Root:** de- (away/from) + port (carry)
- **Chinese:** 驱逐出境 (qūzhú chūjìng)
- **Example:** The government decided to deport the foreign national who violated immigration laws.

---

## Root Summary
- **Latin Root:** port = carry, bear, bring
- **Key Prefixes:** trans-, ex-, im-, sup-, re-, de-
- **Key Suffixes:** -able, -er, -ant
- **Memory Tip:** Think of "port" as carrying something - from importing goods to carrying luggage (porter) to bearing importance (important).
