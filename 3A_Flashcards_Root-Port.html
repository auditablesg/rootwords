🔑 Root Summary

Latin Root: port = carry, bear, bring

Key Prefixes: trans-, ex-, im-, sup-, re-, de-

Key Suffixes: -able, -er, -ant

Memory Tip: Think of "port" as carrying something - from importing goods to carrying luggage (porter) to bearing importance (important).
