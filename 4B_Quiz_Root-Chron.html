Reading Passage

Sarah and Marcus were working on a history project about the American Revolution. They had to arrange the events in chronological order, listing the major battles and diplomatic moments from first to last. This required careful research to ensure the timeline was accurate.

As they read historical documents, they noticed something strange. One source mentioned the colonists using modern technology during the war—a clear anachronism that made the document unreliable. They had to synchronize their sources to separate fact from error. They also used a timeline visualization to help them understand how concurrent events unfolded simultaneously.

To complete their chronicle of the Revolutionary War, they documented each event carefully. The teacher appreciated their effort to maintain chronological accuracy and their ability to identify the anachronism that had confused earlier readers. Their project was a testament to careful historical research.
