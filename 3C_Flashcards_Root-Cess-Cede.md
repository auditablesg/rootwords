# Lesson 3C: Root 'cess' & 'cede' (Go/Yield/Withdraw) - Flashcards

## Card 1: PROCESS
**Front:** Process (noun/verb)
**Back:** 
- **Meaning:** A series of actions or steps taken to achieve a particular end
- **Root:** pro- (forward) + cess (go/step)
- **Chinese:** 过程 (guòchéng)
- **Example:** Writing a good essay is a step-by-step process that requires planning and editing.

---

## Card 2: SUCCESS
**Front:** Success (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** The accomplishment of an aim or purpose; favorable outcome
- **Root:** sub- (under) + cess (go) = "going under to achieve"
- **Chinese:** 成功 (chénggōng)
- **Example:** Hard work and consistent daily revision are the keys to academic success.

---

## Card 3: ACCESSIBLE
**Front:** Accessible (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Able to be reached or easily obtained; easy to use
- **Root:** ad- (to) + cess (go) + -ible (able)
- **Chinese:** 易接近的/可使用的 (yì jiējìn de/kě shǐyòng de)
- **Example:** Ramps and lifts make school buildings fully accessible for students with mobility needs.

---

## Card 4: RECESS
**Front:** Recess (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A brief period of rest between classes or work
- **Root:** re- (back) + cess (go) = "go back/withdraw"
- **Chinese:** 课间休息 (kèjiān xiūxí)
- **Example:** Students eagerly step out of the classroom to play games during short recess.

---

## Card 5: ACCESSION
**Front:** Accession (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** The action of agreeing or consenting; coming into power
- **Root:** ad- (to) + cess (go) + -ion (action)
- **Chinese:** 同意/就职 (tóngyì/jiùzhí)
- **Example:** The accession of the new monarch was celebrated with a grand ceremony.

---

## Card 6: PROCESSION
**Front:** Procession (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A group of people walking in an orderly, formal line
- **Root:** pro- (forward) + cess (go) + -ion (action)
- **Chinese:** 队伍/游行 (duìwu/yóuxíng)
- **Example:** The national day parade featured a colorful procession of marching bands and floats.

---

## Card 7: EXCEED
**Front:** Exceed (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To go beyond what is allowed or expected; to surpass
- **Root:** ex- (out) + ceed (go)
- **Chinese:** 超过/胜过 (chāoguò/shèngguò)
- **Example:** Students should strive not to exceed the word limit in their composition writing.

---

## Card 8: PROCEED
**Front:** Proceed (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To begin or continue a course of action; to move forward
- **Root:** pro- (forward) + ceed (go)
- **Chinese:** 继续/进行 (jìxù/jìnxíng)
- **Example:** After checking all safety rules, the debate team can proceed with their arguments.

---

## Card 9: CONCEDE
**Front:** Concede (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To admit defeat or yield something reluctantly
- **Root:** con- (together) + cede (go/yield)
- **Chinese:** 承认/让步 (chèngrèn/ràngbù)
- **Example:** It takes maturity to admit a mistake and concede when you are wrong in an argument.

---

## Card 10: PRECEDE
**Front:** Precede (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To come or occur before something in time or order
- **Root:** pre- (before) + cede (go)
- **Chinese:** 在...之前 (zài...zhī qiánmiàn)
- **Example:** A detailed introduction must always precede the body paragraphs of an essay.

---

## Card 11: SUCCESSOR
**Front:** Successor (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A person who succeeds another in a position or role
- **Root:** sub- (under/following) + cess (go) + -or (one who)
- **Chinese:** 继任者/继承人 (jìrènzhě/jìchéngrén)
- **Example:** The retiring principal introduced his successor to the entire school during assembly.

---

## Card 12: SECEDE
**Front:** Secede (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To formally withdraw from membership of an organization or alliance
- **Root:** se- (apart) + cede (go/withdraw)
- **Chinese:** 脱离/分离 (tuōlí/fēnlí)
- **Example:** The southern states decided to secede from the Union, which led to the Civil War.

---

## Root Summary
- **Latin Root A:** cess = go, yield, step, proceed
- **Latin Root B:** cede/ceed = go, withdraw, yield
- **Key Prefixes:** pro-, ad-, re-, ex-, pre-, con-, se-
- **Key Suffixes:** -ion, -ible, -or
- **Memory Tip:** Think of "cess/cede" as moving forward or going somewhere - from the process of going through steps to exceeding limits to preceding events.
