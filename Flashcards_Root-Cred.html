CRED Root Summary

CRED (trust, believe) appears in words related to belief, trust, and credibility. When something is incredible, it's hard to believe. A credential proves your trustworthiness. Someone credible deserves your trust. This root helps you understand the connection between concepts of belief, trust, and authentication.
