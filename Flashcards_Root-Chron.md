# Lesson 4B: Root 'chron' (Time) - Flashcards

## Card 1: CHRONOLOGY
**Front:** Chronology (noun)
**Back:** 
- **Meaning:** A record of events in the order they occurred; the science of arranging events by time
- **Root:** chron (time) + -ology (study of)
- **Chinese:** 年代顺序 (niándài shùnxù)
- **Example:** The chronology of historical events helps us understand the order in which they occurred.

---

## Card 2: CHRONICLE
**Front:** Chronicle (noun/verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A historical record of important events; to record events in order
- **Root:** chron (time) + -icle (small)
- **Chinese:** 编年史 (biānnián shǐ)
- **Example:** The historian created a chronicle of the Ancient Egyptian dynasties covering 3000 years.

---

## Card 3: CHRONOLOGICAL
**Front:** Chronological (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Arranged in order of time, from earliest to latest
- **Root:** chron (time) + -logical (relating to)
- **Chinese:** 按时间顺序的 (àn shíjiān shùnxù de)
- **Example:** When we study history in chronological order, we follow events from earliest to latest.

---

## Card 4: SYNCHRONIZE
**Front:** Synchronize (verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** To make things happen at exactly the same time; to coordinate timing
- **Root:** syn- (together) + chron (time) + -ize (to make)
- **Chinese:** 同步 (tóngbù)
- **Example:** During the dance performance, the dancers synchronized their movements with the music.

---

## Card 5: SYNCHRONOUS
**Front:** Synchronous (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Existing or happening at the same time; simultaneous
- **Root:** syn- (together) + chron (time) + -ous (full of)
- **Chinese:** 同时的 (tóngshí de)
- **Example:** In a synchronous online meeting, all participants are connected and communicating at the same time.

---

## Card 6: ANACHRONISM
**Front:** Anachronism (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Something out of its proper time; a historical inaccuracy
- **Root:** ana- (against/backwards) + chron (time)
- **Chinese:** 时代错误 (shídài cuòwù)
- **Example:** Using a smartphone in a movie set in 1920 would be an anachronism because they didn't exist then.

---

## Card 7: CHRONOMETER
**Front:** Chronometer (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A precise instrument for measuring time, especially used for navigation
- **Root:** chron (time) + -meter (measure)
- **Chinese:** 航海钟 (hánghǎi zhōng)
- **Example:** An accurate chronometer on a ship helped sailors navigate and determine their longitude at sea.

---

## Card 8: DIACHRONIC
**Front:** Diachronic (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Looking at something over a period of time; studying historical change
- **Root:** dia- (through) + chron (time) + -ic (relating to)
- **Chinese:** 历时的 (lìshí de)
- **Example:** A diachronic approach to studying language looks at how it changes over time and history.

---

## Card 9: ISOCHRONOUS
**Front:** Isochronous (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Occurring at regular, equal time intervals
- **Root:** iso- (equal) + chron (time) + -ous (full of)
- **Chinese:** 等时的 (děngshí de)
- **Example:** Pendulums are isochronous because they swing at regular, equal time intervals.

---

## Card 10: ASYNCHRONOUS
**Front:** Asynchronous (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Not occurring at the same time; not synchronized
- **Root:** a- (not) + syn- (together) + chron (time) + -ous (full of)
- **Chinese:** 异步的 (yìbù de)
- **Example:** Asynchronous communication allows people to respond at different times without being online simultaneously.

---

## Card 11: SYNCHRONICITY
**Front:** Synchronicity (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** The coincidental occurrence of events at the same time; meaningful coincidence
- **Root:** syn- (together) + chron (time) + -ity (quality)
- **Chinese:** 同步性 (tóngbùxìng)
- **Example:** Meeting my old friend by chance on the same day was an amazing example of synchronicity.

---

## Card 12: ANACHRONISTIC
**Front:** Anachronistic (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Out of place in terms of time; containing historical inaccuracies
- **Root:** ana- (against/backwards) + chron (time) + -istic (having the nature of)
- **Chinese:** 时代错误的 (shídài cuòwù de)
- **Example:** The anachronistic use of modern slang in a Shakespeare play was jarring to the audience.

---

## Root Summary
- **Latin Root:** chron = time
- **Key Prefixes:** syn-, ana-, iso-, dia-, a-
- **Key Suffixes:** -ology, -ic, -ous, -ize, -meter
- **Memory Tip:** Think of "chronological" as arranging events by time - all chron words relate to time, timing, or temporal order.
