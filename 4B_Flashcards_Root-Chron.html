CHRON Root Summary

CHRON (time) appears in words describing the measurement, sequence, and nature of time. Chronology arranges events in time order. A chronometer measures time precisely. Anachronism is something out of its proper time. Synchronize means to occur at the same time. Understanding this root helps you comprehend concepts related to timing, sequences, and temporal relationships.
