# Lesson 4C: Root 'graph'/'gram' (Write/Record) - Flashcards

## Card 1: GRAPH
**Front:** Graph (noun)
**Back:** 
- **Meaning:** A visual representation of data or information using lines, bars, or other symbols
- **Root:** graph (write/draw)
- **Chinese:** 图表 (túbiǎo)
- **Example:** The science class created a graph showing the temperature changes over the past month.

---

## Card 2: GRAPHIC
**Front:** Graphic (adjective)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** Giving a clear, vivid, and often realistic description; relating to visual art
- **Root:** graph (write/draw) + -ic (relating to)
- **Chinese:** 生动的/图形的 (shēngdòng de/túxíng de)
- **Example:** The graphic description of the accident was so vivid that readers could imagine it clearly.

---

## Card 3: PARAGRAPH
**Front:** Paragraph (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A group of sentences about one main idea, usually separated from other groups by indentation
- **Root:** para- (beside/division) + graph (write)
- **Chinese:** 段落 (duànluò)
- **Example:** Each paragraph in an essay typically contains a main idea and supporting details.

---

## Card 4: GRAMMAR
**Front:** Grammar (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** The system of rules for using language correctly; the study of sentence structure
- **Root:** gram (write) + -ar (relating to)
- **Chinese:** 语法 (yǔfǎ)
- **Example:** Understanding grammar rules helps students write more clearly and correctly.

---

## Card 5: TELEGRAM
**Front:** Telegram (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A written message sent by electrical signals through telegraph wires
- **Root:** tele- (distant) + gram (write/message)
- **Chinese:** 电报 (diànbào)
- **Example:** Before email existed, people sent telegram messages through wires for quick communication.

---

## Card 6: AUTOGRAPH
**Front:** Autograph (noun/verb)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A person's own signature or handwriting; to sign one's name
- **Root:** auto- (self) + graph (write)
- **Chinese:** 亲笔签名 (qīnbǐ qiānmíng)
- **Example:** The famous actor signed her autograph on the fan's poster at the event.

---

## Card 7: BIOGRAPHY
**Front:** Biography (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A written account of a person's life; the genre of life stories
- **Root:** bio- (life) + graph (write)
- **Chinese:** 传记 (chuánjì)
- **Example:** I read an inspiring biography of Marie Curie that showed her journey to become a scientist.

---

## Card 8: GEOGRAPHY
**Front:** Geography (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** The study of places, physical features, and peoples of the earth; the layout of a place
- **Root:** geo- (earth) + graph (write/describe)
- **Chinese:** 地理 (dìlǐ)
- **Example:** In geography class, we learned about mountains, rivers, and how people live in different regions.

---

## Card 9: EPIGRAM
**Front:** Epigram (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A short, clever, and witty saying or poem, often with a surprising ending
- **Root:** epi- (upon/at) + gram (write)
- **Chinese:** 警句 (jǐngjù)
- **Example:** An epigram is a short, clever saying that often makes people smile or think.

---

## Card 10: EPITAPH
**Front:** Epitaph (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A written inscription on a tombstone or monument; words honoring the deceased
- **Root:** epi- (upon) + taph (bury)
- **Chinese:** 墓志铭 (mùzhìmíng)
- **Example:** The epitaph on the tombstone read: "Here lies a loving mother and devoted friend."

---

## Card 11: HOLOGRAM
**Front:** Hologram (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A three-dimensional image recorded on a film using laser light; a 3D recording
- **Root:** holo- (whole) + gram (write/record)
- **Chinese:** 全息图像 (quánxī túxiàng)
- **Example:** The hologram technology created a 3D image that appeared to float in mid-air.

---

## Card 12: MONOGRAPH
**Front:** Monograph (noun)
**Back:**
- **Meaning:** A detailed written study or essay on a single, specific subject
- **Root:** mono- (single/one) + graph (write)
- **Chinese:** 专著 (zhuānzhù)
- **Example:** The researcher published a monograph on the history of ancient Roman architecture.

---

## Root Summary
- **Latin Root:** graph/gram = write, record, draw, describe
- **Key Prefixes:** auto-, bio-, geo-, epi-, tele-, para-, mono-, holo-
- **Key Suffixes:** -ic, -phy, -gram, -ph
- **Memory Tip:** Think of "writing" and "recording" - all graph/gram words relate to writing, drawing, describing, or recording information.
