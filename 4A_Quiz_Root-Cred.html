Reading Passage

Marcus Chen was a successful celebrity and televised personality until a journalist discovered he had falsified his academic credentials. The prestigious university he claimed to have graduated from had no record of him ever attending. When this scandal broke, it immediately discredited him in the eyes of the public.

His former fans became incredulous as they learned the truth. How could someone so trusted have lied so brazenly? His credibility had completely vanished. The entertainment industry refused to work with him anymore. What remained of his reputation was destroyed by his own credulity—his willingness to think he could get away with such a deception.

The incident became a lesson in the importance of verifying credentials before granting trust. Many networks now require proof of qualifications from all on-air personalities. The scandal also affected other celebrities, as people became less quick to extend uncritical trust. The public's credulity had been tested and broken.
